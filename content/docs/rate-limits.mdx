# Rate limits

Praxis is a **practice and prototyping** API. There is no marketed public SLA and no guaranteed global quota advertised in the OpenAPI spec.

## What that means

- Do **not** assume unlimited throughput in shared or hosted deployments
- Treat aggressive parallel scraping as abuse even when no `429` is returned locally
- Prefer reasonable client pacing (tens of requests per second is already more than a tutorial needs)

## If you receive `429`

Back off exponentially, reduce concurrency, and cache public fixtures locally when you are iterating on UI.

## Production systems

When you leave the sandbox, design rate limiting, auth, and quotas deliberately. Praxis teaches client shape — it is not a substitute for your production edge.

See also [Errors](/docs/errors) for status handling patterns.
